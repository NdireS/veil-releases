Privacy, honestly

Tested in real calls, not just promised.

We shared a screen with Veil open and watched from a second laptop. Veil also checks your own PC and tells you if it can't hide there.

What Veil can't hide from: a phone or camera pointed at your screen, or someone in the room. Recordings and the Zoom desktop app haven't been tested yet. Do a quick test call in your own meeting app before you rely on it.
